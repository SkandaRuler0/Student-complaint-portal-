Welcome to the Student Complaint & Feedback Portal

This portal allows students to report problems, submit complaints and provide feedback about their college experience.

What Can You Report?

  • Classroom problems
  • Laboratory problems
  • Wi-Fi or internet issues
  • Library problems
  • Hostel issues
  • Campus cleanliness
  • Other college-related problems

How Does It Work?

  1. Fill out the complaint form.
  2. Submit your complaint.
  3. The concerned department reviews it.
  4. The problem is investigated.
  5. The complaint is resolved.
